About Me

As a mom of two—one of whom has special needs—I learned firsthand how important life insurance really is. My husband and I realized we needed to protect our children’s future if something happened to either of us. That experience is what led me to become an insurance agent: first to protect my own family, and then to help other families do the same.

I’m passionate about making protection simple and personal. Whether you need life insurance, mortgage protection, coverage for your children, retirement solutions, or a program to help you get out of debt sooner, I’ll walk you through options that actually fit your life and budget. I work with more than 30 carriers and can often find coverage with no medical exam.

Getting protected doesn’t have to be complicated—and it can give your family real peace of mind when they need it most.
